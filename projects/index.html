What makes a project useful?

01

A reproducible environment

Document the architecture, versions, assumptions, scope, and setup so the work can be repeated.

02

Evidence with context

Show what was tested, what happened, and why it matters. Separate confirmed findings from hypotheses.

03

A verified improvement

Explain the remediation and retest the original condition. Record limitations and remaining risk.
