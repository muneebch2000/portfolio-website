# Finding Template

## Finding
Short title.

## Risk
Explain the security impact.

## Preconditions
Describe the controlled lab conditions required.

## Reproduction
Document only the steps needed to reproduce inside SecureRAG.

## Evidence
Screenshots, API responses, logs, or test output.

## Root cause
Application, retrieval, data, model, or policy failure.

## Mitigation
Specific engineering control.

## Retest
Pass/fail result after the fix.

## Residual risk
What remains after remediation.
